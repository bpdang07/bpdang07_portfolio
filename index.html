01 — About me

Code to cloud, end to end.

I'm a software engineer who likes the whole path from code to cloud: writing the feature, wiring up the pipeline, and watching it land safely in production. I studied Software Engineering at Iowa State University (B.S., Class of 2026).

Right now I'm a Full Stack Developer Intern at VetsEZ, building features for VA-facing healthcare software used by veterans across the U.S., working with AWS and CI/CD pipelines in a government cloud environment. Before that I spent two years at Railpark Thoughts, a startup where I went from game development and research to deploying and managing projects on Google Cloud.

Outside of work I'm usually solving my daily LeetCode problem, building something in Godot, or at a hackathon. My team's app Mazi won Best AI Application at Iowa State's Applied AI hackathon.
